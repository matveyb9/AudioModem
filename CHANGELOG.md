# Privacy and data handling

AudioModem is designed to work locally. The initial project does not require an account or a backend service to encode or decode a transfer.

Audio carried through a selected route may be heard, recorded or processed by equipment in that route. A WAV export contains the encoded signal and must be treated like the original object when sharing it. The project does not claim confidentiality until a future reviewed encryption mode exists.

Callsigns are optional display labels and may be visible in an unencrypted transfer. Do not use a callsign as evidence of identity. Received objects stay in temporary local storage until integrity verification succeeds and the user elects to save them.
