# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

We commit to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background, device ownership, language or communication style.

## Expected behaviour

Use constructive language, focus criticism on ideas and code, respect private boundaries, give and accept feedback professionally, and ask for evidence when technical claims matter.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, publication of private information, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and sharing recordings or files without permission are unacceptable. Do not use project spaces to request unlawful radio operation or to evade legal restrictions.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to maintainers. Maintainers may request changes, issue warnings, limit participation or remove content and access. Reports are handled as confidentially as practical.
